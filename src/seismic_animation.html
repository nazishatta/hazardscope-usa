Density shows the geographic concentration of recorded events, not earthquake probability, shaking intensity, risk, or observed damage. Weighted mode sums (max(0, magnitude)+1)² for each earthquake as an illustrative emphasis; this is not seismic energy or a calibrated hazard score. Time playback accumulates events from the fetched window. Changing magnitude filters the same fetched records; to include smaller events, lower the USGS minimum-magnitude setting and Fetch again. Geographic outlines are approximate context; state selection uses the USGS query bounding box.
